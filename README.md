## 🧰 What does DynaTech do?

DynaTech is a broad Slimefun addon full of machines and quality-of-life technology. It intentionally mixes practical automation with unusual and experimental ideas.

### ⚙️ Machines & automation

- **Auto-Kitchen** — automatically crafts supported kitchen recipes.
- **Growth Chambers** — automated plant growth with support for compatible garden content.
- **Antigravity Bubble** — powered temporary flight within an area.
- **Weather Controller** — changes weather using key items.
- **Potion Sprinkler** — applies potion effects over an area.
- **Barbed Wire** — pushes mobs away from an area.
- **Material Hive** — bee-powered resource generation.
- **Wireless Charger** — charges rechargeable items near the machine.
- **Seed Plucker** — extracts seeds from supported plant materials.
- **Orechid** — converts stone/netherrack into ores.
- **Wireless Energy Bank / Point** — wireless energy storage and transfer.
- **Wireless Item Input / Output** — one-way wireless item transfer.
- **Tesseract** — two-way item and energy transport.
- **External Heater** — heats furnace-like blocks externally.

### ⚡ Generators

Hydro Generator, Dragon Egg Generator, Chipping Generator, Culinary Generator, and Stardust Reactor provide alternative ways to produce Slimefun energy.

### 🧰 Tools & utilities

DynaTech also includes the Picnic Basket, Electrical Stimulator, Inventory Filter, Angel Gem, Scoop, Dimensional Home, Tesseract Binder, Portable Fluid Tank, and other utility items.

### 🐝 Bees & Item Bands

Natural and robotic bees improve Material Hive production, while Item Bands can add special effects such as extra health or haste to equipment.

### 🔌 Optional integrations

DynaTech can expose additional content when compatible addons such as Infinity Expansion are installed. Integration availability depends on the exact addon versions present on the server.

## 🧪 Slimefun Legacy maintenance

This fork keeps DynaTech's original content recognizable while maintaining compatibility with the modern Slimefun Legacy stack. Existing item IDs, recipes, machines, and integrations should be preserved wherever practical during compatibility work.

Back up established worlds and plugin data before replacing any production build.
